Client Database Cleanup

DJD Realty kept its leads in three places: website inquiry forms, a Facebook Page Messenger sheet and open house sign-up sheets typed in from paper. I combined all 254 rows in Excel, fixed every field, merged the duplicates and delivered one clean list of 212 clients and leads, so every agent works from the same list and no hot lead slips through.

8How I cleaned it

  1. Kept the original safe.

    Stacked the three sheets on one tab with a row number and source reference, then left it untouched so every change can be traced back.

  2. Set one format per field.

    TRIM and PROPER for names, LOWER for emails, SUBSTITUTE to strip phone symbols and peso signs, a formula to turn “3.5M” and “15k” into full numbers, DATE to rebuild day-first dates, and lookup tables for areas, client types, property types, stages and agent initials.

  3. Found duplicates in three passes.

    Same email, then same mobile, then same name and city. Each match was checked with COUNTIF before anything was merged, and two rows were never merged if their emails or numbers disagreed.

  4. Merged without losing history.

    Inquiries were added up, the earliest inquiry and latest contact kept, the most advanced stage kept, and every merge recorded in the Duplicate Log.

  5. Measured the result.

    Scored completeness, validity, consistency and uniqueness on the raw sheet and on the clean list, so the improvement is a number, not an opinion.

  6. Protected it going forward.

    Dropdowns for area, client type, property type, stage, agent and consent, date and number rules, a pipeline status formula, and a follow-up queue for gaps.
