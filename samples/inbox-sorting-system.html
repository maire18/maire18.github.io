2Label structure

LabelWhat goes hereExampleCleared
01 ActionNeeds a reply, decision or task from the DirectorApproval requests, client questionsDaily
02 WaitingSent and waiting on someone elseQuotes, signed contractsChecked Mon and Thu
03 Read laterWorth reading, never urgentNewsletters, industry reportsFriday
04 ReferenceKept for records, no actionPolicies, signed agreementsArchived
05 ReceiptsInvoices, bookings and payment confirmationsFlight and hotel confirmationsFiled monthly

Numbered names keep the labels in priority order in the sidebar.

3Filter rules

When a message…Then
Has “approval”, “urgent” or “sign” in the subjectApply 01 Action, star, mark important
Comes from a key client domain (list of 12)Apply Clients, never send to spam, mark important
Comes from invoices@, billing@ or a booking siteApply 05 Receipts, skip the inbox
Contains “unsubscribe”Apply 03 Read later, skip the inbox
Is a calendar invitationKeep in inbox, apply Calendar

4Daily triage routine

  1. Three sessions a day: 8:30 AM, 12:30 PM and 4:30 PM, twenty minutes each.
  2. Do it now if the reply takes under two minutes.
  3. Delegate to the right person and label 02 Waiting.
  4. Defer to 01 Action with a due date in the task list.
  5. Archive everything else. The inbox ends each session empty.

5Friday reset

  • Follow up on anything in 02 Waiting older than 5 days
  • Move finished threads to 04 Reference
  • Unsubscribe from lists that went unread for a month
  • Update the key client list for filters
  • Share a short summary of open items with the Director

6Reply templates

Meeting request

Subject: Re: Meeting with [Name]

Hi [Name], Thank you for reaching out. [Director] is available on [Day, time] or [Day, time]. Which works best for you? I will send a calendar invite once confirmed. Best regards, [Your name]

Invoice received

Subject: Re: Invoice [Number]

Hi [Name], We received invoice [Number] and passed it to our finance team for processing. Payment follows our standard [30-day] terms. I will reach out if anything else is needed. Thank you, [Your name]

Holding reply

Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name], Thank you for your message. [Director] is traveling this week, and I have flagged your email for review. You can expect a full reply by [Date]. Kind regards, [Your name]
