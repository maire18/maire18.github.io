Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This website, maire18.github.io, is the online portfolio of Mary Rose Obsuna, a virtual assistant based in Buhi, Camarines Sur, Philippines. This policy explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can do about it. I keep it simple because I collect very little.

1. Information I collect

Contact form. If you send a message through the contact form, I receive the details you type in: your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. Please do not include sensitive information such as passwords, bank details or government ID numbers.

Email. If you email me directly at mairrmdd@gmail.com, I receive your email address and whatever you write.

Analytics data. The site uses Google Tag Manager, which may load analytics tools such as Google Analytics. These collect general usage information, for example the pages you view, how you arrived at the site, your approximate location (city or country level), device and browser type, and how you interact with the page (such as clicking a link or submitting the form). They do this using cookies or similar technologies and your IP address, which Google may process.

2. How I use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss possible work together.
  • To understand how the site is used so I can improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

I do not sell your information, and I do not use it for advertising.

3. Who handles your information

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers contact form messages to my inbox. Your message passes through their service.
  • Google (Tag Manager, Analytics and Gmail) processes analytics data and the emails I receive.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log standard technical data such as IP addresses when pages are requested.
  • Third-party links. Links to LinkedIn, OnlineJobs.ph and other sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

These providers may process data outside the Philippines, including in the United States.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. The analytics tools above use them to recognize repeat visits and measure usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site still works if cookies are blocked.

5. How long I keep it

I keep messages and emails only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of our conversation or working relationship, and delete them when they are no longer needed. Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings for my account.

6. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, and similar laws in other countries, you may ask me to:

  • tell you what information I hold about you,
  • correct information that is wrong,
  • delete your information or stop using it, and
  • withdraw your consent where I rely on it.

Email mairrmdd@gmail.com and I will respond as soon as I can. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you may also contact the Philippine National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and I limit access to messages to my own email account. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

This site is meant for business use and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
